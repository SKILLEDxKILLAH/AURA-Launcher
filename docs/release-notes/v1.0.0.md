The first release of **AURA**, a minimalist home screen for **Android 17** with Google Gemini built in.

## Install
1. Download **`AURA-Launcher-1.0.0.apk`** below and open it on your phone. Allow installing from your browser or file manager if Android asks.
2. Press **Home** and choose **AURA**, or open AURA → **Settings → Set AURA as your home app**.
3. To use Gemini, follow **`GEMINI_SETUP.md`** (attached below and in [`docs/`](https://github.com/SKILLEDxKILLAH/AURA-Launcher/blob/main/docs/GEMINI_SETUP.md)).

Requires Android 17 (API 37) and Google Play services.

## What's in 1.0.0
**Home**
- A large clock and date, plus weather under the clock or a Pixel "At a Glance" style weather line at the top.
- The top-left text is editable: tap it to write your own. Long-press it for settings.
- A Gemini card and a dock of your default apps.
- Press and hold for **Wallpaper & style** and **AURA settings**.
- Swipe down with one finger for notifications, two fingers for Quick Settings.

**App drawer**: swipe left
- A big serif section letter and one search bar for apps, settings, Gemini and the web.
- **Suggested / Recent** apps, learned on the phone from when and how often you open them.
- The **curved A–Z scroller**: drag the letters and they bend toward your finger. Slide onto any app and let go to open it.
- Options to hide the drawer's Gemini bar and button.

**News feed**: swipe right
- Picture cards from BBC News, The New York Times and The Guardian, plus Ars Technica and The Verge for tech.
- Weather with today's high and low, topic tabs, and **Brief me**, where Gemini sums up the headlines.

**Gemini, inside the launcher**
- **Continue with Google** once, or use an API key. The Gemini app never opens.
- Gemini can operate your phone: flashlight, alarms, timers, calls, message drafts, navigation, apps, settings, media and calendar drafts.
- Pick a model (Best / Balanced / Fastest, or any model your account has) and a **thinking level** from Auto to High.
- Inline voice input, and a built-in **Gemini check** with a copyable report.
