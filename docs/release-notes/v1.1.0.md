## AURA 1.1.0

**New**
- **Widgets:** press and hold the home screen → **Widgets**. Widgets sit between the clock and Gemini. Hold one to resize, move or remove it.
- **App shortcuts:** hold any app for its shortcuts ("New message", "Navigate home"…), plus favorites, hide, App info and Uninstall. Works in the drawer, suggestions, search and the dock.
- **Themes:** Dark, Light or Match phone. Choose AURA's sand colors or your wallpaper's colors (Material You), and optional themed icons.
- **Gemini writes as it thinks:** replies and "Thinking…" appear word by word.
- **Friendlier app drawer:**
  - List, Compact or Grid layouts.
  - The big letter shrinks as you scroll.
  - Hide apps (they're still in search).
  - A tips card, and an option to open the keyboard right away.
- **Report** under every Gemini reply.
- Gemini starts a new chat each time you open it (from 1.0.1).

**Privacy and Play readiness**
- AURA no longer asks to see every installed app. It asks only for apps with icons or widgets.
- Your list of apps is no longer sent to Gemini; app names are matched on the phone.
- Google sign-in asks only for Gemini access, not your whole Google Cloud.
- A clear, separate explanation appears before turning on AURA gestures.
- Your Gemini API key is kept out of cloud backups.
- Weather uses a location rounded to about 1 km.

Installs over 1.0.x and keeps your settings and sign-in.
