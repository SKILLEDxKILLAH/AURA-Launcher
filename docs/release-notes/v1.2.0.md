## AURA 1.2.0

**Gemini without Google Cloud**
- **On this phone:** on phones that support Gemini Nano, Gemini runs on the device with one tap. No account, no key, and nothing leaves the phone. AURA itself handles everyday commands like timers, alarms, the flashlight, calls, texts, directions, music, settings and opening apps.
- **Free Gemini key, guided:** tap **Open Google AI Studio** → **Create API key** → **Copy**, come back and tap **Paste my key**. AURA checks the key instantly. No Cloud console and no card.
- **Continue with Google** is still there for testers you've added.
- Change how Gemini runs any time in **Settings → Gemini → How Gemini runs**.

**New**
- **Welcome tour** on first launch: getting around, making AURA your home app, picking a look, setting up Gemini.
- **Choose your dock:** hold any app → **Add to dock**.
- **Calculator in search:** type "15% of 80" or "12*7" and tap the answer to copy it.
- **Contacts in search,** with call and text buttons (needs Contacts access).

Installs over 1.1.x and keeps your settings and sign-in.
